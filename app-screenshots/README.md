# Ebookvox: Google Play store assets

Built from the real app (the Reader), its logo, and its colours (brown #3a261c / #6b4a3a, peach #ee9b6e, cream #fbf5ec), with the Fraunces and Inter fonts.
The screens show real books from the Ebookvox library and a demo account.

| Folder | Size (px) | Google Play field | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| `app-icon/` | 512 × 512, PNG | App icon | `app-icon-512x512.png` ← upload this one |
| `feature-graphic/` | 1024 × 500 | Feature graphic | `.png` or `.jpg` (no transparency) |
| `phone/` | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Phone screenshots (2–8) | 6 images |
| `tablet-7-inch/` | 1200 × 1920 (5:8) | 7-inch tablet screenshots | 6 images |
| `tablet-10-inch/` | 2560 × 1600 (16:10) | 10-inch tablet screenshots | 5 images |
| `desktop/` | 1920 × 1080 (16:9) | Chromebook / desktop screenshots | 5 images |
| `android-xr/` | 2560 × 1440 (16:9) | Android XR screenshots | 5 images |

Order: the number at the start of each file name is the order to upload them (first = shown first).

Notes
- The icon is a full square. Google Play rounds the corners itself. `app-icon-512x512-rounded-preview.png` only shows how it looks rounded; `app-icon-1024x1024-source.png` is the same icon larger, for other stores.
- The Android XR images show the app as a flat panel floating in space. They are a store graphic, not a capture from an XR headset.
- Tablet and desktop images are the website layout (the app loads the same screens), shown in a tablet or browser frame.
